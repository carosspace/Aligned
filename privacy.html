Privacy Policy — Aligned

Last updated: 14 August 2026

Aligned ("the app") is a personal planner published by Anima Temple ("we", "us"). This policy explains what the app does with your information. In short: the app works locally by default, and if you choose to sign in, your planner is backed up to our server so you can sync it across devices. We run no advertising, analytics, or tracking, and we never sell your data.

1. Data stored on your device

Everything you enter into Aligned — tasks, habits, cycle logs, journal entries, notes, birthdays, money entries, fitness logs, bucket-list items, astrology preferences, your name and settings — is stored locally on your device. If you never sign in, it stays only on your device and is never sent to us. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data permanently deletes this local copy.

2. Optional cloud backup & sync (only if you sign in)

Aligned offers an optional backup and cross-device sync. It is off until you choose to sign in with your email address and a 6-digit PIN. If you do sign in:

  • What is stored: your email address, your PIN (stored only as a secure one-way hash — we never store the PIN itself), and your planner data. That planner data can include sensitive information you choose to enter, such as menstrual-cycle and health logs, fitness logs, personal journal entries, and personal finance entries.
  • Where: on servers operated by Anima Temple, used solely to provide your backup and sync. Your data is not shared with any third party.
  • In transit: all communication with our server uses encrypted HTTPS.
  • Sign-in tokens: when you sign in, a random access token is stored on your device to keep you signed in. Signing out removes it.
  • Deletion: you can have your account and all server-stored data deleted at any time by emailing us at the address below. Uninstalling the app removes the local copy from your device.

3. Optional AI assistant

Aligned includes an optional assistant. If, and only if, you enter your own third-party AI API key and use that feature, the text of your prompt is sent from your device directly to that third-party provider (Anthropic, api.anthropic.com) to generate a response. Your API key is stored only on your device; we do not receive it or your prompts. Your use of that provider is governed by their own privacy policy. If you don't use the feature, nothing is sent.

4. What we do not do

  • No advertising, and no advertising identifiers.
  • No third-party analytics or tracking SDKs.
  • We do not sell or share your data with third parties.
  • The app requests no location, contacts, camera, or microphone access.

5. Children

Aligned is intended for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

6. Changes

We may update this policy. Material changes will be reflected by the "Last updated" date above.
